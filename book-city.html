Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking stays, tables, tickets and offers in the City of London.

How do I book a hotel, restaurant, attraction or show?

Pick a category from the Book It Please… tiles above, or jump straight to a section — Stays, Restaurants, Attractions or Shows. Each listing links through to real-time availability so you can complete your booking in a few clicks.

Is the Things to do carousel updated regularly?

Yes — the highlights across stays, dining, attractions, shows and more are refreshed regularly, so it’s worth checking back if you’re planning a visit. You can also see the full list on our Experiences page.

Can I cancel or change a hotel booking?

Cancellation policies vary by hotel and rate — look for the “Free cancellation” or “Non-refundable” tag on each listing before you book. Once booked, changes and cancellations are managed from your confirmation email.

Do I need to book restaurant tables in advance?

We’d recommend it, especially for popular spots like Duck & Waffle and Sushisamba London or around lunchtime and weekends. Reserving ahead guarantees your table at the listed price.

Are attraction tickets valid on any date?

Most attraction tickets are date-flexible unless stated otherwise, but time-slot attractions such as Sky Garden require you to reserve a specific slot in advance. Check the ticket details at checkout for each venue.

Who do I contact if I have a problem with my booking?

Your confirmation email includes the details of the operator or venue handling your booking. For general questions about Book City, visit our Contact Us page.
